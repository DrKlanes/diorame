Super Riso ink names and colours come from [riso-colors](https://github.com/mattdesl/riso-colors) by Matt DesLauriers, used under the [MIT license](https://github.com/mattdesl/riso-colors/blob/master/LICENSE.md).

Super Riso textures (`public/textures/superRiso/`) are original to Diorame. `ink_black.png` and `ink_red.png` (ink film and micro-voids) and `ink_lut.png` (thickness inversion LUT) were synthesised from statistics MEASURED on real riso prints (power spectrum and thickness histogram of the ink film; density, size, shape and clustering of the voids): the film by IAAFT (iterative amplitude-adjusted Fourier transform), the voids by a Thomas cluster point process. They contain no pixels from any third-party scan or image, and tile seamlessly. `greennoise1024.png` is generated procedurally. The paper fibres and inclusions are generated at runtime.
